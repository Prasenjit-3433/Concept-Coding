# Moss (getmoss.com) — Company Overview

---

## What the Platform Does

Moss is a **spend management platform** for small and medium-sized businesses (SMBs). The core idea is simple: most SMBs manage company money across four separate, painful workflows — issuing cards to employees, tracking expenses, handling incoming supplier invoices, and reimbursing out-of-pocket employee costs. Moss puts all four into a single dashboard.

Today, Moss powers over 5,000 SMEs across Europe with a platform that unifies spend management — from corporate cards to reimbursements to accounts payable. Customers use Moss to streamline operations, gain real-time visibility, and shift finance from reactive admin to proactive strategy.

The four product pillars:

**1. Corporate Cards** — Physical and virtual cards on the Mastercard network. Admins can issue a physical card for each employee or team, and virtual cards for online payments and subscriptions. Cards are credit cards (not debit/prepaid), meaning you don't top up — you spend within a limit and settle monthly.

**2. Expense Reimbursements** — Employees who pay out of pocket submit claims digitally. The mobile app lets them snap receipts, assign cost centers and VAT, and the claim goes through an approval workflow automatically.

**3. Accounts Payable** — Companies can digitally enter and approve incoming invoices, with all modules benefiting from accounting automation and integrations with common accounting software and ERP systems.

**4. Payments ("Pay with Moss")** — Instant reimbursements for employees and payments to local suppliers via SEPA Instant Credit Transfer, plus the ability to pay global suppliers in over 70 currencies.

All of this feeds into accounting integrations — mainly DATEV (Germany-specific), Xero, NetSuite, and 40+ others — so that by month-end, most of the bookkeeping is already done.

## Scale & Maturity

Moss launched in mid-2020 as the first real corporate credit card for startups and digital companies in Germany. So it's roughly 5 years old.

Current scale signals:

- As of October 2025, Moss has approximately 446 employees across offices in Berlin, London, and Amsterdam.
- Serving 5,000+ SMEs across Germany, Netherlands, and UK.
- 2024 revenue was approximately $19.38M.
- 100% ARR growth in the last 12 months (measured mid-2024), with 120% net revenue retention. (120% NRR means existing customers are expanding their spend on Moss, not just staying.)
- Nearly 1,000 businesses now rely on Moss's proprietary AI as an integral part of their accounting workflows.

This puts Moss in **mid-stage startup territory** — past the "does this even work?" phase, but still growing hard and not yet at the "running on autopilot" scale of a public company.

**Core — Stable and Production-Ready:**

- Corporate cards (physical + virtual), spend limits, real-time transaction tracking
- Receipt capture and OCR via mobile app
- Accounts payable: invoice ingestion, approval workflows, payment runs

## Business Model

**Who they sell to:** SMBs with roughly 10–500 employees, particularly finance-forward companies — tech startups, agencies, and professional services firms that have outgrown spreadsheets but aren't large enough to justify enterprise ERP systems.

**Revenue streams (how they actually make money):**

Moss has two main revenue levers:

1. **SaaS subscription fees** — Companies pay a monthly fee per module (cards, AP, reimbursements). The platform is modular, so customers can start with just cards and add more later.
2. **Interchange fees** — Every time a Moss card is swiped, Mastercard pays a small percentage of the transaction back to the card issuer (Moss). This is the same model Brex/Ramp use in the US. At scale, transaction volume makes this meaningful.
3. **Interest/credit income** — Cards work on a credit basis — spend within a limit, pay at the end of the month. Moss holds the credit risk and earns from this (partially why the €50M debt round was needed — to fund the loan book).
4. **Cashback as a retention tool** — Every time customers make a purchase, they receive 0.4% cashback on all expenses, which is a customer acquisition/retention mechanism, not a revenue line.

**Value Proposition (plain language):**
For a 50-person company, the finance person used to chase paper receipts, reconcile a shared company card, manually enter supplier invoices into DATEV, and run reimbursements through bank transfers. Moss replaces all of that with one platform and automates the accounting export. The pitch is hours saved per month and fewer month-end surprises.

## Competitors

Moss sits against: **Pleo** (Denmark, most direct), **Spendesk** (France), **Payhawk** (Bulgaria/UK), **Yokoy** (Switzerland), and internationally **Brex/Ramp** (US equivalents). The key differentiator historically was being credit-based (not prepaid), which gives higher effective limits and suits companies that don't want to pre-load cash.
